Frequently asked questions

How does the diagnostic fee work? +

The diagnostic covers my time to find out what's wrong. If you go ahead with the repair, the fee is credited toward the total. If you decide not to, the fee still applies since it covers the time and travel.

Do you come to me, or do I send it in? +

Either. I make house calls across South-Central PA. If you're outside the area, or just prefer it, you can mail your device in, and I'll repair it and ship it back.

How does a mail-in repair work? +

Send me a request first and I'll walk you through packing and shipping. I'll diagnose it and send you a quote. Once the repair is done and the balance is paid, I ship it back to you.

What if it can't be fixed? +

You'll hear that from me during the diagnostic, before any repair begins, along with your options. No repair work is done without your OK.

Is the repair guaranteed? +

Yes. Every repair comes with a 30-day workmanship guarantee.

How do I pay? +

I accept Zelle, cash and check. I send a simple invoice, and the diagnostic fee is collected before we schedule a call or visit.

What areas do you serve? +

House calls across South-Central PA. If you're outside the area, mail-in repairs are available from anywhere.
